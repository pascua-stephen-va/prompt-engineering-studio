Core Principle 01

Anatomy of a Prompt

Name the persona, the task, the limits, and the output format.

01
Persona and Task

Tell it who to be

The persona is the expert you want answering. Define the role and exact objective clearly from the first sentence.

"Act as a social media manager for a Cebu coffee brand."
02
Context and Limits

Fence the output

Give audience, tone, length, and hard limits. Vague briefs force the model to invent the details you left out.

"Target young remote workers, warm conversational tone, max 120 words, no hashtags."
03
Format and Example

Show the shape

Name the output format, such as a table, five bullets, or a 30-second script, and paste one sample to match.

"Output as a 3-column table (Scene, Voiceover, Visual) matching this sample..."

          Core Principle 02
          

            Positive and Negative Prompts
          

          

            Positive prompts declare what must appear. Negative prompts remove what keeps showing up uninvited.
          

        


        

          

            

              1
              Positive
            

            
State the wins

            

              List subject, style, lighting, and composition in plain language. Specific nouns outperform adjectives every time.
            

          


          

            

              2
              Negative
            

            
Exclude the noise

            

              Add a negative list for recurring faults: extra fingers, watermarks, blurry text, cluttered backgrounds.
            

          


          

            

              3
              Priority
            

            
Weight what matters

            

              Put the one detail that defines the shot first. Most models read earlier terms as more important.

            Freestyle Storytelling Blueprint
          
          

            50-Second Cinematic Video Structure (5 Scenes · 10s Each)
          

        

        
      


      

        Every scene generated by your AI Tool in Task 3 follows this 5-beat storytelling arc and outputs four reusable pieces: A. Voiceover (12–20 words), B. Text on Screen (ALL CAPS, ≤8 words), C. Text to Image Prompt, and D. Negative Prompt.
      


      

        

          
SCENE 1 · HOOK (0 to 10 sec) · Medium Shot

          
Establish your chosen setting, introduce your character exactly as built in Task 1 & 2, and open with a scroll-stopping hook.

        

        

          
SCENE 2 · CONTEXT (10 to 20 sec) · Focused Detail

          
Show the character's daily routine, goal, or central conflict through a closer visual detail.

        

        

          
SCENE 3 · RE-HOOK (20 to 30 sec) · Movement / Traversal

          
Introduce a turning point, obstacle, surprise twist, or shift in perspective to hold attention mid-video.

        

        

          
SCENE 4 · VALUE (30 to 40 sec) · Intimate Close-Up

          
Reveal the deeper meaning, emotional realization, or core takeaway of your story.

        

        

          
SCENE 5 · REWARD (40 to 50 sec) · Wide Atmospheric Shot

          
Resolve the story with your chosen outcome—triumphant, unexpected, reflective, or open-ended—and a memorable final line.
